VIDEO POKER RULES

JACKS OR BETTER: Standard 5-card draw video poker.

DEAL: You get 5 cards. Choose which to HOLD, then DRAW replacements.

PAYOUTS: Royal Flush 250:1, Straight Flush 50:1, Four of a Kind 25:1, Full House 9:1, Flush 6:1, Straight 4:1, Three of a Kind 3:1, Two Pair 2:1, Jacks or Better 1:1.

STRATEGY: Always hold a paying hand. Hold high pairs (J+). Draw to flushes and straights.
