Drop the artworks here as:

    1.png   2.png   3.png

## Tournament hall (2 pieces) — priced in tournament TOKENS
Drop the artworks here as:

    t1.png   t2.png

(Any square-ish image works; PNG or JPG — match the extension in the catalog.)
Walk up to a piece and press A to view it, read the blurb, then tip the
artist or buy the piece.

Titles, artists, payout wallets, prices, blurbs and token IDs are set per-slot
in the `GALLERY` (floor) and `TGALLERY` (tournament) catalogs near the top of
`games/clubnile.html`. Until an image is uploaded, a placeholder frame shows in
its place.
